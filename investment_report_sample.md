## Top 3 Stock Picks — Ranked by Conviction

### 1. 🏆 NVDA — NVIDIA Corporation
**Conviction Level:** High  

**Investment Thesis**  
NVIDIA maintains an unassailable moat in AI training and inference hardware, commanding an estimated 80–90% share of the high-performance GPU market. Its CUDA ecosystem creates extraordinary switching costs, while the Blackwell architecture cycle positions the company for another earnings supercycle beginning H2 2025. Revenue growth of 122% YoY in the latest fiscal year reflects demand that continues to outstrip supply.

**Key Catalysts to Watch**
- Blackwell ramp: data centre shipments accelerating through Q3/Q4 2025
- Sovereign AI deals (Middle East, Europe) — each worth $1B+
- GTC 2026 announcements: next-gen Rubin architecture reveal

**Main Risks**
- U.S. export restrictions on advanced chips to China
- Customer concentration (hyperscalers represent ~50% of revenue)
- Potential AMD / custom ASIC market share erosion over 24–36 months

**Entry Consideration**  
Accumulate on pullbacks toward the $850–$900 support zone. Earnings as a catalyst trigger.

### 2. 🥈 AMD — Advanced Micro Devices
**Conviction Level:** High  

**Investment Thesis**  
AMD's MI300X GPU is gaining traction as enterprises seek NVIDIA alternatives amid supply constraints. With a credible AI hardware roadmap and strong server CPU momentum, AMD is positioned to capture incremental AI infrastructure spend. The stock trades at a meaningful discount to NVIDIA on forward P/E despite a comparable growth profile.

**Key Catalysts to Watch**
- MI350 / MI400 roadmap execution
- Microsoft, Meta, and Oracle GPU procurement diversification
- Continued server CPU market share gains vs. Intel

**Main Risks**
- Execution risk on GPU software ecosystem (ROCm vs. CUDA gap)
- Demand concentration risk if NVIDIA supply eases significantly
- Margin pressure from aggressive pricing to win design wins

**Entry Consideration**  
Favorable entry below $175; strong support at $155. Watch for Q2 earnings beat as a re-rating catalyst.

### 3. 🥉 AVGO — Broadcom Inc.
**Conviction Level:** Medium–High  

**Investment Thesis**  
Broadcom is emerging as a critical beneficiary of the custom silicon (XPU) wave, with Google TPUs and Meta's MTIA chips built on Broadcom designs. Its AI revenue segment has grown explosively, and its infrastructure software business (VMware integration) provides durable recurring revenue and margin stability. The combination of custom AI chip growth and software revenue makes it a uniquely defensive AI play.

**Key Catalysts to Watch**
- New XPU design wins (rumoured Apple, OpenAI partnerships)
- VMware cross-sell to enterprise clients materialising
- Dividend growth — Broadcom is one of few AI plays with a strong yield

**Main Risks**
- VMware integration execution complexity
- Customer concentration (Google ~20% of AI segment revenue)
- Premium valuation limits upside if AI capex slows

**Entry Consideration**  
Attractive accumulation zone at $1,550–$1,650. Long-term holders can consider buying at market given the quality of the franchise.
